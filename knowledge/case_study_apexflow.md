# Case Study: ApexFlow

Document Type: Internal Case Study
Data Status: Fictional / Demo Data

> NOTE: ApexFlow is a fictional client of the fictional company Northstar Labs. All
> information in this case study is fictional and exists solely to demonstrate the
> Northstar Labs sales intelligence MVP. It must not be presented as real-world companies
> or a real client relationship.

## Client

ApexFlow

## Industry

B2B SaaS

## Company Size

Approximately 280 employees

## Project

AI-Powered Account Research and Sales Qualification Platform

## Background

ApexFlow is a fictional B2B SaaS company providing workflow management software to
mid-market organizations.

As ApexFlow expanded its enterprise sales organization, its sales development
representatives were required to research an increasing number of target accounts.

Before contacting an account, representatives manually reviewed:
- Company websites
- Recent company announcements
- Hiring activity
- Product information
- Executive information
- Industry information
- Potential business signals

## Problem

The manual research process took approximately 18 minutes per account.
The sales team also experienced inconsistency in the quality of account research.
Different representatives would prioritize different signals and record information
differently.

This created three problems:
1. High manual research time.
2. Inconsistent account qualification.
3. Limited scalability as the sales team grew.

## Northstar Solution

Northstar Labs designed an AI-assisted account research system.
The workflow collected publicly available company information and converted it into a
structured account brief.

## System Workflow

Company -> Research -> Signal Extraction -> ICP Matching -> Research Brief -> Human Review

The system identified signals such as:
- Hiring
- Funding
- Product launches
- Geographic expansion
- Partnerships
- Technology changes

The signals were compared against ApexFlow's internal ideal customer profile.
The system then generated a structured research brief for the sales representative.

## Human Review

The system did not automatically contact prospects.
A sales representative reviewed the generated research before using it for outreach.
This allowed the team to maintain human judgment while reducing repetitive research work.

## Technology

The project used:
- Python
- FastAPI
- LLM-based extraction
- Retrieval-Augmented Generation
- Vector search
- REST APIs
- PostgreSQL
- CRM integration

## Results

Following implementation:
- Average account research time decreased from approximately 18 minutes to approximately
  4 minutes.
- Sales representatives could research more accounts during the same working period.
- Account research became more standardized.
- Managers gained greater visibility into why accounts were being prioritized.

## Business Impact

The primary value came from reducing repetitive research work while preserving human
review. The system allowed sales representatives to spend more time evaluating
opportunities and engaging prospects rather than manually collecting information.

## Relevance to Future Prospects

This case study is particularly relevant when a prospect demonstrates:
- Rapid sales hiring
- Expansion into new markets
- Increasing account volume
- Manual prospect research
- Sales qualification bottlenecks
- CRM data-quality challenges
- Repetitive account research
