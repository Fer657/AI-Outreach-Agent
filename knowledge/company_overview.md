## Company Overview

Northstar Labs is a fictional B2B AI engineering and automation consultancy focused on
helping growing technology companies reduce manual operational work and build intelligent
internal systems.

Northstar works primarily with B2B SaaS, FinTech, and technology-enabled businesses
experiencing rapid growth, increasing operational complexity, or fragmented data workflows.

## Company Mission

Northstar Labs helps companies turn repetitive knowledge work into measurable,
AI-assisted workflows while keeping humans involved in important decisions.

## Core Services

1. AI Automation
   Northstar designs AI-powered workflows that automate repetitive knowledge-intensive
   processes. Capabilities include:
   - AI workflow automation
   - LLM-powered applications
   - AI agents
   - Document processing
   - Intelligent classification
   - Human-in-the-loop workflows
   - Automated research

2. Sales Intelligence
   Northstar helps sales teams reduce the amount of manual research required before
   prospecting. Capabilities include:
   - Account research
   - Lead qualification
   - Prospect enrichment
   - ICP matching
   - Sales signal detection
   - Automated research briefs
   - CRM enrichment
   - Outreach intelligence

3. RAG Systems
   Northstar builds retrieval-augmented generation systems that allow AI applications to
   use a company's internal knowledge. Typical sources include:
   - Internal documentation
   - Case studies
   - SOPs
   - Product documentation
   - Knowledge bases
   - Customer information

4. Data Engineering
   Northstar develops data pipelines and systems that bring information from multiple
   sources into a structured environment. Capabilities include:
   - ETL pipelines
   - Data integration
   - Data cleaning
   - Analytics
   - API integrations
   - Data quality systems

5. AI Product Engineering
   Northstar builds custom AI-powered applications. Examples include:
   - Internal AI assistants
   - Research platforms
   - Recommendation systems
   - Intelligent dashboards
   - AI-powered SaaS features
   - Workflow agents

## Industries

Northstar primarily targets:
- B2B SaaS

## Ideal Customer Profile

Northstar is best suited for companies with:
- 50–1000 employees
- Rapid growth
- Significant amounts of manual knowledge work
- Growing sales or operations teams
- Fragmented information
- Repetitive research processes
- Existing software systems that need intelligent automation

## Common Business Triggers

Northstar typically investigates companies experiencing:
- Rapid hiring
- Geographic expansion
- New product launches
- Funding events
- Increasing sales volume
- Increasing operational complexity
- Manual research bottlenecks
- Data fragmentation
- Repetitive customer or sales workflows

## Typical Engagement

A Northstar engagement generally follows:
1. Discover the operational problem.
2. Identify the existing workflow.
3. Quantify the manual effort or business impact.
4. Design an automation architecture.
5. Build the AI/data system.
6. Integrate with existing tools.
7. Keep humans involved in important decisions.
8. Measure the resulting business impact.

## Positioning

Northstar does not position AI as a replacement for employees.
The company's approach is to automate repetitive work while allowing employees to review,
approve, and make important decisions.
