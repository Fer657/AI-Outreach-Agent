# Northstar Labs — Capability Matrix

Document Type: Internal Knowledge Base
Data Status: Fictional / Demo Data

> NOTE: Northstar Labs is a fictional company. All information in this document is
> fictional and exists solely to demonstrate the sales intelligence MVP.

## Sales Intelligence

### Account Research Automation
Northstar can build systems that automatically collect publicly available information about
target companies and convert it into structured research briefs. Relevant when a sales team
spends significant time researching accounts manually.

### Lead Qualification
Northstar can build AI-assisted qualification systems that compare prospects against an
organization's ideal customer profile. Relevant signals include:
- Company size
- Industry
- Technology
- Hiring
- Growth
- Geography
- Product activity
- Business events

### Sales Signal Detection
Northstar can identify signals such as:
- Hiring
- Funding
- Expansion
- Product launches
- Partnerships
- Acquisitions
- Executive announcements

These signals can be used to prioritize accounts or personalize sales research.

## AI Agents

Northstar develops specialized AI workflows for:
- Research
- Classification
- Document analysis
- Data extraction
- Qualification
- Recommendation
- Content generation

Agents can be connected into multi-step workflows where each stage produces structured
output for the next stage.

## RAG

Northstar builds RAG systems that retrieve relevant internal information before generating
an answer. Typical architecture:
Documents -> Chunking -> Embeddings -> Vector Search -> Retrieval -> LLM -> Response

RAG is useful when a company needs AI to answer questions using proprietary information.

## Automation

Northstar can automate workflows involving:
- CRM updates
- Data enrichment
- Research
- Document processing
- Reporting
- Internal knowledge retrieval
- Repetitive administrative work

## Data Integration

Northstar can integrate:
- REST APIs
- CRM systems
- Databases
- Internal applications
- Web data
- Business tools

## Best-Fit Problems

Northstar has the strongest potential fit when a prospect has:
1. A clearly identifiable repetitive workflow.
2. Significant manual effort.
3. Structured or semi-structured data available.
4. A measurable business outcome.
5. A workflow where AI can assist without removing human oversight.
